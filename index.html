New Age Home Business Profiles

New Age Home by ECHON is a one-stop destination for premium PVC and WPC building materials, delivering a next-gen factory-to-floor experience across Southeast Asia. Established in 2024 as the retail initiative of ECHON, the manufacturing brand of Kumar Arch Tech Ltd., New Age Home brings factory-direct interior solutions to architects, interior designers, builders, contractors, fabricators and homeowners.
