New Age Home Business Profiles

New Age Home by ECHON is a one-stop destination for premium PVC and WPC building materials, delivering interior solutions for architects, interior designers, builders, contractors, fabricators and homeowners. Established in 2024 as the retail initiative of ECHON, New Age Home brings factory-direct interior solutions to customers and professional project buyers. The product range includes MasterBoard, Venya wall and ceiling panels, SafeStrong doors and frames, PVC flooring and cabinetry materials with product guidance and bulk project supply from the Udaipur experience centre.
